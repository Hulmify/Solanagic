# Chrome Web Store Listing for Solanagic

## Title
Solanagic: AI-Powered Solana Wallet

## Short Description (Summary)
Manage assets and transact on Solana using natural language with our smart AI assistant. The next-gen AI wallet.

## Detailed Description

**Solanagic** is a next-generation crypto wallet for the Solana blockchain, revolutionizing how you interact with your digital assets. By integrating Google's advanced **Gemini AI**, Solanagic transforms your wallet into a smart, conversational assistant.

Forget navigating complex menus. Simply chat with your wallet to check balances, analyze transaction history, or send funds. Whether you are a developer testing on Devnet or a user managing assets on Mainnet, Solanagic makes the experience seamless, intuitive, and powerful.

### 🌟 Key Features

**🤖 Smart AI Assistant**
*   **Natural Language Control**: Ask "How much SOL do I have?" or "Show my last 5 transactions" and get instant answers.
*   **Actionable Commands**: Instruct the AI to "Send 0.1 SOL" or "Get an airdrop," and it will prepare the transaction for you.
*   **Context-Aware**: The AI understands your wallet's specific context, offering personalized insights.

**💼 Complete Wallet Management**
*   **Create & Import**: Generate new wallets in seconds or import existing ones using standard seed phrases.
*   **Send & Receive**: specific interfaces for sending SOL and copying your address.
*   **Transaction History**: Detailed logs of your recent activities with direct links to the Solana Explorer.

**🌍 Multi-Network Support**
*   **Developer Friendly**: Seamlessly switch between **Mainnet Beta**, **Devnet**, and **Testnet**.
*   **One-Click Airdrop**: Instantly request Devnet SOL for testing and development.

**🔒 Secure & Private**
*   **Local Storage**: Your private keys and mnemonic phrases are encrypted and stored locally on your device.
*   **User-Controlled API**: You provide your own Gemini API Key, ensuring you have full control over the AI integration.

**🎨 Modern & Sleek Design**
*   Enjoy a beautiful, dark-themed interface designed for clarity and ease of use.

### 🚀 Getting Started
1.  Install the extension.
2.  Create a new wallet or import an existing one.
3.  Click the "Sparkles" icon to open the AI Assistant.
4.  Enter your Gemini API Key (free tier available from Google).
5.  Start chatting with your wallet!
